You've had hundreds of conversations with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. AegisMem finds the one you need in a single search, and carries any chat to another AI in one click. Private: everything stays encrypted on your device.

Add to Chrome (free)How it works

One search, every AI

Search by words or meaning across all your chats. Filter by app or date. Jump straight to the message.

Your whole history on day one

Import the official exports from ChatGPT, Claude and Google (Gemini). New chats are saved automatically.

Continue anywhere

Hit a usage limit, or want a second opinion? Carry a chat's context to another AI in one click. You review before sending.

Private by design

No account. No servers. Encrypted on your device. The extension's pages can't even connect to the internet.

Why you can trust it

WhatHow
Where your chats liveOnly in your browser, encrypted with AES-256. An optional passphrase is available.
Who can read themYou. We run no servers, so there is nothing for us to see. AI websites never get access to your history.
When anything leaves your deviceOnly when you click "Continue in…" or "Ask…". The text then goes to the AI you chose, after you press Enter.
Secrets in your chatsAPI keys and card numbers are masked before saving.
Can you verify it?The extension's network block is enforced by Chrome itself (Content Security Policy), and automated tests check it on every build. Anyone can inspect the extension's files after installing it.

Honest numbers

On a public benchmark (LongMemEval) with about 10,000 conversations, AegisMem puts the right chat in the top 5 for 56% of questions and responds in about 0.1 seconds. Meaning-based search ties keyword search on that benchmark, so we don't claim it beats it there. It helps when you don't remember the exact words. Full method and caveats are in the README.
